Sunhaven Streets — a sun-soaked 2D top-down open-world crime game, built from scratch for make.fun's "Make GTA 6" bounty. You are Alex Vega, a driver new in the coastal city of Sunhaven with a debt to clear. Explore a living city on foot and by car, steal cars, outrun the Sunhaven police across a 5-star wanted system, fight the Red Tide gang, and run 6 story missions — from your first stolen wheels, through a checkpoint run across the bay, to the big bank score — to become the King of Sunhaven.

The game includes a full open world (road grid, buildings with rooftop clutter, parks, parking lots, a beach, boardwalk and bay, traffic AI with brake lights, pedestrians who stroll, flee and phone in crimes), car theft and arcade driving with a handbrake drift that lays skid marks, police chases with predictive intercepts and arrests, fist and pistol combat where bullets stop at walls and gang members shoot back, two walk-in shops (ammo at Coral Market, hot meals at the Sunset Cafe), first-aid pickups, a HUD with health, money, wanted stars, a heat-cooling bar, the district name and a live minimap with landmark letters, synthesized WebAudio sound effects and music (no audio files, no network), a start screen, pause, and localStorage saves. Everything is original: our own city, characters, story, art and sounds.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080
```

Controls: WASD move / drive · E enter or exit a car (steal it) · F punch · G shoot (aim with the mouse) · Shift run on foot / handbrake drift in a car · B buy at a shop door · P pause · H save · C continue a saved game · M mute music. Or open `game/index.html` from any static server — the game is plain static files with no build step and no network requests.
